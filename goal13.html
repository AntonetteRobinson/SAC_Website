Sustainable Development Goal 13

Climate Action

Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts. Climate change affects communities, ecosystems and economies across the world.

Learn More

The Challenge

Why Climate Action Matters

Rising temperatures, extreme weather and environmental damage create challenges for both people and nature.

Goal 13 encourages urgent action to reduce climate change, improve resilience and support a more sustainable future.

Key Areas

Climate change

Extreme weather

Carbon emissions

Environmental resilience

The Impact

Who and What Does It Affect?

People

Climate change can affect health, homes, food supplies and livelihoods.

Nature

Changing temperatures can damage habitats and threaten wildlife and biodiversity.

Our Future

Decisions made today will influence the environment inherited by future generations.

TAKE ACTION

What Can We Do?

Individual actions alone cannot solve climate change, but sustainable choices can support wider environmental efforts.

01 Reduce unnecessary energy use.

02 Choose sustainable transport where possible.

03 Reduce waste and reuse materials.

04 Support environmental initiatives.
